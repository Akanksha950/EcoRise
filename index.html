Our Story

Rooted in Purpose, Growing Towards a Greener Future

EcoRise Foundation was born in 2009 from a simple belief: that every child deserves to understand the living world they inhabit, and every community deserves the tools to protect it.

What started as weekend ecology workshops in Jharkhand has grown into a nationwide network of educators, volunteers, and changemakers united by one mission — making sustainable living the norm, not the exception.

We partner with government schools, tribal communities, and urban neighborhoods to deliver hands-on environmental education that is joyful, relevant, and deeply local.

🌿 Ecological Literacy

Teaching children to read the natural world around them.

🤝 Community First

Solutions that emerge from and return to communities.

🔬 Science-Backed

Curriculum grounded in rigorous environmental science.

💡 Youth Agency

Empowering young people as environmental leaders.

What We Do

Six Programs.
One Planet.

Each program is carefully designed for its audience — from curious 8-year-olds to community leaders — with measurable outcomes and local adaptation.

🏫

GreenSchools Initiative

A 40-week ecology curriculum integrated into state board schools, covering biodiversity, water cycles, climate science and sustainable agriculture.

K-12 Education
🌾

Tribal Wisdom Project

Documenting and reviving indigenous ecological knowledge in partnership with Adivasi communities across Jharkhand and Odisha.

Community
💧

Watershed Warriors

Training local youth as water auditors and river monitors, using low-cost tools to track river health in real time.

Water Conservation
🌳

Urban Forest Lab

Transforming barren school grounds into thriving micro-forests using the Miyawaki method, with students as lead ecologists.

Urban Ecology
♻️

Zero-Waste Campus

Helping schools and colleges achieve zero-waste status through composting, upcycling workshops, and systemic waste audits.

Circular Economy
🦋

Young Naturalists Club

After-school biodiversity monitoring clubs where children contribute real data to national citizen science projects.

Citizen Science
